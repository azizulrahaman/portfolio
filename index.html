About Me

Focused on detection: finding the attack in the logs and writing it up so the next analyst can act on it.

I hold an M.S. in Cybersecurity and Network Administration from Touro University (2026), a B.S. in Computer Science from University Geomatika Malaysia, and the CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) certification.

In my home lab I run Splunk Enterprise, attack Windows and Active Directory targets, triage the alerts, and write incident reports mapped to MITRE ATT&CK. I'm building an attack-to-detection pipeline next: attack VulnHub targets, forward the logs, write SPL detections, and publish the write-ups.

My research looks at ML-based intrusion detection that scores well in testing and fails quietly in practice. I'm available for all shifts, including evenings, overnights, and weekends, and open to relocating anywhere in the US.
